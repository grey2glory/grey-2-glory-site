Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Grey 2 Glory is a community app for miniature painters. This policy explains what information the app keeps, who can see it, who helps us run it, and how you can remove it. It’s written in plain language on purpose. Throughout, “we” means the owner of Grey 2 Glory.

What we collect

Your account

  • Email and password, if you sign up with email. Your password is stored only in protected, scrambled form; we never see it.
  • Sign in with Apple: Apple gives us an account ID, and the email and name you choose to share. You can use Apple’s “Hide My Email” option, in which case we only see a private relay address.

Your profile

Your handle, army or faction, bio, profile picture and any social media usernames you add.

What you share

Posts and their photos, progress updates, tags, paints used, recipes, comments, likes, saved posts and recipes, and your votes on paint comparisons.

Your hobby tracking

Your Pile of Shame (including when you mark models as painted, which counts toward your painting streak), your paint rack, wishlist and paints marked as running low, and the badges you’ve earned.

Connections and messages

The painters you’ve added, and direct messages. Messages are encrypted on your device before they’re sent, so only you and the person you’re talking to can read them. We store the encrypted message and when it was sent, but we can’t read its contents.

Safety and settings

Painters you’ve blocked, reports you send, your notification and privacy choices, and which messages and notifications you’ve already seen.

What we don’t collect

Grey 2 Glory doesn’t collect your location or contacts, doesn’t use advertising or tracking tools, and doesn’t track you across other apps or websites. We don’t sell or rent your information to anyone.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera and photo library access only so you can photograph and share your minis or set a profile picture. It only uses the photos you choose.

Who can see what

  • Other signed-in painters: your profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, recipes, badges, the painters you’ve added, and your votes on paint comparisons. Your Pile of Shame, paint rack and wishlist, and painting streak are visible too unless you hide them in your privacy settings.
  • Only you: your email address, saved posts and recipes, blocked list, reports you’ve sent, and private settings.
  • Only the two people in a conversation: the words in your direct messages.
  • The app owner: reports sent about posts, comments, recipes or painters, so they can be reviewed.

Photos you post are stored at public web addresses so they can be shown in the app. Anyone who has the exact address of a photo could open it, so only post photos you’re happy to share.

How we use your information

Only to run Grey 2 Glory: to show your profile and posts to the community, power features like the Hall of Glory, badges, streaks and paint matching, deliver your messages, keep the community safe, and reply when you contact us.

Who helps us run the app

  • Supabase hosts the app’s database, photo storage and sign-in.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers the app through the App Store and TestFlight.
  • Expo provides the tools used to build and update the app.

These companies process information only to provide their services to us, under their own privacy policies.

Keeping the community safe

Posts, comments, recipes and profiles are checked for blocked words before they’re shared. You can report or block any painter. The app owner reviews reports and may remove content or accounts that break the community rules.

Your choices and deleting your information

  • You can edit your profile, and delete any of your posts and comments, at any time.
  • You can hide your Pile of Shame, paint rack and wishlist, and painting streak from other painters in your privacy settings.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you’ve shared, including photos, posts, comments, likes, your pile, paint rack, connections and messages.
  • Prefer email? Write to us and we’ll delete your account for you.

We keep your information only while your account is open. When you delete your account, it’s removed from the app straight away. Copies in our hosting provider’s backups are cleared on its normal backup schedule.

Children

Grey 2 Glory isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version here with a new effective date, and let you know in the app if the change is significant.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information? Email grey2glory.app@gmail.com.
