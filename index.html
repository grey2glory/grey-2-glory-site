⚜ Ways to support Grey 2 Glory ⚜

The Gallery

Post your minis, finished or work in progress. Add progress updates, compare before and after, zoom in on the details and cheer each other on.

Pile of Shame

Log the kits you haven’t painted yet, sort them by army, roll the dice to pick your next project, and climb the Hall of Glory.

Paints

Step-by-step recipes with colour swatches, community-voted matches across brands, and a paint rack and wishlist that shows which recipes you can paint.

Badges and streaks

Earn 17 heraldic medals, from bronze seals to gold laurels, keep a weekly painting streak burning, and show them off on your profile.
