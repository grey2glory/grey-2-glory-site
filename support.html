Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email grey2glory.app@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

If you’re reporting a bug, it helps to include a screenshot, what you were doing when it happened, and your iPhone model.

Common questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app and tap Sign in with Apple, or create an account with your email and a password. Then pick a handle, which is the name other painters see.

I signed up with email but can’t sign in

Check your inbox (and spam folder) for the confirmation email and tap the link inside. The link may open a page that can’t be reached. That’s fine: your account is confirmed. Go back to the app and sign in.

I forgot my password

Email us from the address you signed up with and we’ll help you get back in.

How do I report a post, comment or painter?

Tap the ⋯ button on the post, or the ⋯ next to the comment, and choose Report. Reported content is hidden for you straight away, and the app owner reviews every report.

How do I block someone?

Tap the ⋯ on any of their posts or comments and choose Block. You won’t see their posts or comments, and they can’t message you. They aren’t told. You can unblock painters from your Profile tab.

How do I hide my Pile of Shame, paint rack or streak?

Use the privacy settings in your profile. Your posts, comments, recipes and badges always stay visible to the community.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you’ve shared. You can also email us and we’ll do it for you. See the privacy policy for details.
