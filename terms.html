Community rules and terms

Effective October 21, 2026

Grey 2 Glory is a place to celebrate miniature painting and collecting. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to these rules and terms. If you don’t agree, please don’t use the app.

Community rules

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Don’t post, comment or message anything that:

  • harasses, bullies, threatens or targets anyone;
  • is hateful or discriminatory, including slurs or attacks based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other identity;
  • is sexual, pornographic or sexualises anyone, including any content involving minors;
  • shows or encourages real-world violence, self-harm or illegal activity;
  • is spam, a scam, advertising you weren’t invited to share, or a fake account;
  • shares someone else’s personal information, such as their address or phone number;
  • uses photos or painting work that isn’t yours without permission and credit.

Keep it friendly. Critique the paint job, never the painter. Grimdark settings are welcome; real-world cruelty isn’t.

Reporting and enforcement

You can report any post, comment or painter from the ⋯ menu, and block anyone. The app owner reviews reports, normally within 24 hours, and may remove content and suspend or delete accounts that break these rules, with or without warning.

Your content

  • You keep ownership of the photos and words you post.
  • By posting, you give Grey 2 Glory permission to store, display and share that content inside the app, and to show it in screenshots that promote the app, for as long as it’s posted. Delete a post and this permission ends for that post.
  • Only post content you have the right to share.

Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old, and old enough to use the App Store where you live.
  • Keep your sign-in details safe. You’re responsible for what happens on your account.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete my account.
